Expert decision framework

B.L.A.S.T. keeps the logic understandable: Bias, Location, Action, Safety, and Targets.

Transparent signal model
B — BiasEMA trend reveals whether structure is bullish, neutral, or bearish.
L — LocationPrice is judged inside its 7-day range so entries are not blindly chased.
A — ActionBUY, HOLD, or SELL is chosen only when multiple conditions align.
S — SafetyVolatility creates a risk level and informs invalidation distance.
T — TargetsEvery coin gets an entry zone, stop logic, and profit target.
