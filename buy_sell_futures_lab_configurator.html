Designed around the three useful short frames

Use 15m for the trigger, 30m for confirmation, and 1h for the bigger structure. The app shows all three so users do not blindly overtrade one candle.

15-minute trigger

Best for immediate action, but noisy. The lab treats it as a trigger, not the whole decision.

30-minute confirmation

Filters fake moves by checking whether short-term momentum has follow-through.

1-hour structure

Keeps high-leverage traders from fighting the broader candle direction.
