Introduction

Pakistan's First
Cyber Security Academy

Welcome to the National Cyber Security Academy (NCSA) at Air University — the first-of-its-kind initiative in Pakistan dedicated to advancing education, training, and research in Cyber Security.

Our journey began in 2018 with the launch of Pakistan's pioneering undergraduate program. Today, NCSA has evolved into a comprehensive Academy with four schools, a training center, and a state-of-the-art cyber range.

We host the National Center for Cyber Security (NCCS), supported by 11 specialized labs across Pakistan.

Message from the Dean

Dean, NCSA
Prof. Dr. Zunera Jalil
Dean, National Cyber Security Academy
20+ years academic experience · FAST-NUCES

Welcome to the National Cyber Security Academy (NCSA) at Air University, the first-of-its-kind initiative in Pakistan dedicated to advancing education, training, and research in Cyber Security.

With state-of-the-art facilities, globally benchmarked curricula, and a strong emphasis on experiential learning, the Academy stands as a hub for cultivating the next generation of Cyber Security professionals, researchers, and innovators.

Our vision is to position Pakistan as a global leader in Cyber Security education, research, and industry collaboration. We are committed to building a world-class workforce, advancing curriculum excellence, driving sustainable economic growth, and achieving global leadership.

As Dean of NCSA, I warmly invite you to join us in shaping a safer and more secure digital future for Pakistan and beyond.
